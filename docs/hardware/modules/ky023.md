## ADC values

The ESP32-S3 uses a 12-bit ADC (0–4095). The center is **not exactly 2048** — the ADC has known non-linearity and the potentiometer is not precision-grade. On this module the resting values sit around **1860–1900** for both axes. Use auto-calibration at startup (see sketch 12).

## Orientation and axis swap

The module is rectangular and can be mounted in 4 orientations. The reference is the position of the module's **labels/silkscreen**. In the natural orientation (0°) the labels face left.

A mount rotation changes **two** things at once: which potentiometer maps to the horizontal/vertical axis (**swap**) and whether pushing "right" raises or lowers the ADC reading (**polarity**). The old model (swapping `JOY_X_PIN`/`JOY_Y_PIN` + "invert" flags) used two separate knobs that had to agree by hand — fragile and error-prone.

### Model: center first, then rotate

The wiring is **fixed**: VRx is always on GPIO2, VRy always on GPIO1. Orientation is a **single knob**, `JOY_MOUNT`:

1. Read the fixed pins and subtract the calibrated center → signed deltas `rvx`, `rvy`. Centering first turns inversion into a simple sign flip (no more "0→4095 becomes 4095→0").
2. `applyMount()` maps `(rvx, rvy)` into the **world** frame (`+x = right`, `+y = down`). A single matrix resolves swap and polarity together, so they can never drift out of sync.

Everything downstream (the 8-direction `atan2`, or raw `dx`/`dy` for analog expression/pitch-bend) uses the world frame.

| Orientation | Labels face | `dx` | `dy` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ROT_0` natural | left | `−rvx` | `−rvy` |
| `ROT_90_CW` | up | `−rvy` | `+rvx` |
| `ROT_90_CCW` ← **test board** ✅ | down (toward you) | `+rvy` | `−rvx` |
| `ROT_180` ← **likely final device** | right | `+rvx` | `+rvy` |

> The table is **anchored to the hardware-verified case** (`ROT_90_CCW`: `dx=+rvy, dy=−rvx`, confirmed — X correct, Y not inverted). The other three are derived as a consistent rotation (each row turns 90° from the previous; opposite rows negate each other), but are **not hardware-confirmed** — they depend on the internal potentiometers' direction. Verify with sketch 12 when remounting and, if any axis comes out mirrored, negate that axis in the corresponding `applyMount()` case.

**Current configuration (test board):**

```cpp
#define VRX_PIN    2   // VRx — fixed
#define VRY_PIN    1   // VRy — fixed
#define JOY_MOUNT  ROT_90_CCW
```

**If the final device mounts the module at 180°** (labels facing right), just change the knob — the wiring stays untouched:

```cpp
#define JOY_MOUNT  ROT_180
```

## Direction detection

Sketch 12 uses `atan2` on the (dx, dy) vector to determine 8 directions. This prevents a dominant movement on one axis from being canceled by noise on the other — if the lever is pushed fully down and nudged slightly right, it keeps reporting DOWN until the angle exceeds 22.5° from the vertical axis.

The threshold is computed as a percentage of the available range from the calibrated center (`THRESHOLD_PCT`, 25% by default).
