# Pocket Groovebox — Design & Concept Exploration

Visual exploration of what the device could look and feel like. These are **concept
renders, not specs** — they explore form factor, layout, and personality. The product
direction lives in [brief.md](brief.md#product-concept); component reality lives in
[hardware.md](hardware/README.md).

## Concepts

### 01 — Two-level handheld layout (top-down)

<details>
<summary>Prompt</summary>

```text
A clean industrial product design sketch of a portable handheld synthesizer
on white background, top-down view (bird's eye view) with slight 3D
perspective to show depth, pencil and marker style with subtle shading.

Device dimensions: approximately 20 cm wide × 9 cm tall × 3.5 cm deep.
Handheld console form factor with TWO-LEVEL DESIGN: an elevated raised
section on top for controls (about 5mm higher) and a lower flat section
below for the keyboard. Inspired by Teenage Engineering EP-133 K.O. II
and Playdate aesthetic.

ALL EDGES ARE ROUNDED with consistent proportional corner radius:
- Device outer corners: ~8-10mm radius (soft, hand-friendly)
- Elevated section corners: ~6mm radius
- Display bezel corners: ~3mm radius
- Speaker grille: perfect circle with soft edges

ELEVATED TOP SECTION (raised ~5mm above base):
- TOP-LEFT (positioned about 3cm from left edge, near display): a chunky
  round rotary encoder knob with knurled black matte aluminum cap, ~2.2 cm
  diameter, with push-button click, labeled "PARAM 1"
- TOP-CENTER: rectangular IPS color display with rounded bezel corners,
  1.9 inch diagonal, 320×170 banner format (3.8cm × 1.9cm). Shows synth UI:
  waveform on top half, parameters below (CUT: 800, RES: 45, ATK: 12, REL: 340)
- TOP-RIGHT (positioned about 3cm from right edge, mirroring left): another
  chunky black knurled aluminum knob, ~2.2 cm diameter, with push-button
  click, labeled "PARAM 2"
- Subtle thin orange horizontal line at the bottom edge of the elevated
  section (10% accent color), visually dividing controls from keyboard zone

LOWER BASE SECTION (flat):
- BOTTOM-LEFT: small analog thumb joystick with black rubber cap, ~1.5 cm
  diameter, labeled "MOD X/Y"
- BOTTOM-CENTER: 1 octave mini keyboard (13 keys: 8 white + 5 black).
  CHUNKY SHORT TOY-PIANO style keys (Oskitone Scout inspired), not slim
  professional mini-keys. White keys are cream-colored matching body.
  Black keys are short matte black blocks.
- BOTTOM-RIGHT: circular speaker grille with pattern of small round holes
  (vintage radio aesthetic), ~3 cm diameter, in light gray color

COLOR PALETTE (60/30/10 rule):
- 60% DOMINANT: Warm cream / off-white (#F5F2E8) for the main body of both
  levels, like vintage Japanese hi-fi or EP-133 K.O. II body
- 30% SECONDARY: Light warm gray (#C8C5BE) for the elevated section's top
  surface around the display, the speaker grille area, and panel borders
- 10% ACCENT: Vibrant warm orange (#FF6B35) used sparingly for: a small
  embossed logo on the elevated section, the thin horizontal divider line,
  small accent rings at the base of each knob, and a tiny power LED indicator

FUNCTIONAL BLACK COMPONENTS:
- Knobs: matte black knurled aluminum
- Joystick cap: matte black rubber
- Black piano keys: matte black
- Display when off: deep black

VISIBLE DETAILS:
- Silver/chrome screws at the four corners of the elevated section
  (Monome Norns Shield aesthetic)
- Soft drop shadow beneath the elevated section to emphasize the two-level
  design
- Subtle texture suggesting 3D-printed plastic or soft-touch coating

SIDE DETAILS (show as small inset diagrams or annotations):
- BACK: USB-C port right, small slide switch ON/OFF left
- RIGHT SIDE: thumbwheel volume control wheel partially exposed, labeled "VOL"
- FRONT-LEFT EDGE: 3.5mm headphone jack labeled "PHONES"

OVERALL VIBE: pastel-toned, modern-retro, warm and inviting like a vintage
cassette deck meets handheld game console. References: Oskitone Scout (toy
piano keys, vibrant 3D-printed feel), Teenage Engineering EP-133 K.O. II
(two-tone cream/gray with orange accents, elevated panel), Playdate (cute
handheld form, vibrant identity), Monome Norns Shield (visible screws,
premium feel), Hichord (joystick + keyboard layout), Valve Steam Deck
(ergonomic two-hand grip).

Style: clean product design illustration with thin pencil/ink lines and
subtle marker shading. Include small annotations with arrows labeling each
component. White or very light gray background.
```
